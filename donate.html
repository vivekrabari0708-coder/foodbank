Food Safety Guidelines
  • Food must not exceed 4-6 hours from cooking time unless properly chilled.
  • Must be stored in clean, covered containers or leak-proof packaging.
  • Keep raw and cooked foods separated.
  • Packaged items must be within their labeled expiration dates.
