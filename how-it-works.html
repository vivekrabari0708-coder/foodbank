Step-by-Step Architecture

How FoodBank Works

Our technology bridges excess food supply from donors directly with verified grassroots hunger-relief networks in an efficient 4-step pipeline.

Step 1 2 Minutes

Donate Food

When wedding banquet halls, corporate cafeterias, bakeries, or families have surplus edible meals, the donor accesses the FoodBank donation portal.

  • Enters estimated meal servings & food category
  • Specifies cooking time and preferred pickup window
  • Instant geolocation tagging for swift volunteer routing
Step 2 Real-time Match

FoodBank Connects Donors & NGOs

The platform broadcasts the availability to our network of registered NGOs, community kitchens, and volunteer teams closest to the pickup address.

  • Automated notification sent via SMS & Web app
  • Matches quantity with shelter capacity
  • NGO accepts request within 15 minutes
Step 3 Safe Transit

Volunteer / NGO Collects Food

Trained volunteers arrive in insulated containers or delivery vans to inspect, pack, and transport the surplus food safely.

  • Temperature and hygiene verification checklist
  • Safe handling in food-grade carriers
  • Handoff acknowledged digitally
Step 4 Mission Accomplished

Food Reaches People In Need

Within 60-90 minutes of pickup, warm nutritious meals are served to homeless shelters, child welfare homes, and slum communities.

  • Distributed with dignity and care
  • Donor dashboard gets updated with photos & metrics
  • Zero edible food wasted in landfills
