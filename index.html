How FoodBank Works

Connecting generous food donors with local non-profits in just four easy steps.

01

1. Donate

Individuals, caterers, or restaurants list their surplus edible food with quantity and pickup address.

02

2. Connect

Our smart network instantly alerts verified local NGOs, volunteers, and hunger-relief organizations.

03

3. Collect

NGO volunteers arrive at the location, verify freshness guidelines, and collect the food packaging safely.

04

4. Share

Hot meals are delivered with dignity to community shelters, orphanages, and underprivileged families.

Our Impact & Purpose

Why FoodBank?

We bridge the gap between food abundance and severe hunger with technology.

Reduce Food Waste

Over 40% of food prepared at weddings and banquets is discarded. We redirect perfectly good food from landfills.

Help Communities

Ensure children, senior citizens, and daily wage workers get nutritious meals whenever food is surplus.

Easy Food Donation

Donating takes less than 2 minutes on our mobile-ready website. No cumbersome red tape or long approvals.

Build a Better Future

Every meal saved lowers greenhouse gas emissions and fosters a culture of empathy and community care.
