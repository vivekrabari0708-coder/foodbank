About FoodBank

Bridging the Divide Between Surplus Food and Hunger

Founded with a singular mission: to make sure no edible food ends up in trash cans while people in our surrounding neighborhoods sleep hungry.

Zero Food Waste

Transforming surplus into warm, nourishing meals.

Community Dignity

Providing clean, safe, respectful food assistance.

Top Non-Profit Tech Initiative 2026

Our Mission

To eliminate edible food waste by establishing an instant, tech-enabled redistribution network connecting households, restaurants, caterers, and institutions with trusted local charitable organizations.

  • Rapid collection within 1-2 hours of notification
  • Strict hygiene and food safety quality checks
  • Transparent tracking from donor handoff to recipient

Our Vision

To build a society where no individual goes to bed hungry while surplus food is being incinerated or dumped in landfills, fostering a sustainable circular food economy driven by compassion.

  • Zero-hunger cities powered by smart community sharing
  • Drastic cut down on municipal greenhouse emissions
  • Active youth and volunteer civic participation
